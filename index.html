How this works: The tool compares the pasted document against the relevant standard template using an LLM to identify substantive deviations versus cosmetic ones, and classifies each as a pattern you've likely seen before (low/medium — usually fine to approve or use a known response) or genuinely novel and needing a lawyer's eyes (high). In single mode it's a second pair of eyes while you stay in control of every document. In batch mode it triages a pile so you only open what actually needs you — the rest gets a short reason logged against it, which doubles as an audit trail for why something was waved through.
